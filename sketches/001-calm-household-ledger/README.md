## Variant: Calm Household Ledger

### Design stance
Warm, household-first finance dashboard: easier to read, less cyber/AI, more like a trusted money notebook.

### Inspiration
- Wise design language: lime accent, confident fintech typography, pill CTAs.
- Notion-style warm neutrals: paper-like surfaces, low visual noise.
- Behance finance-dashboard search: personal finance dashboards often lead with balance, cashflow, and recent transactions.

### Key choices
- Layout: desktop sidebar + two main content cards.
- Typography: large but friendly headline, readable Inter.
- Color: warm cream, green accent, minimal dark surfaces.
- Interaction: quick-add buttons show a toast.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: daily usability, non-technical feeling, portfolio-friendly trust.
- Weak at: less “power cockpit” for advanced users.

### Best for
A RumahBudget redesign aimed at ordinary daily finance tracking and recruiter-friendly product clarity.
